## 5. DOS Sub-Scene Consolidation & Economy Scaling Ledger

To preserve every mandatory causal gate and branch of `SNR3`–`SNR6` while fitting Remake's port/building loop and fame/gold economy scale, the following explicit consolidations and numeric scalings are applied:

1. **Otto (`SNR3`)**:
   - **Starting Gold & Palace Commission (`O0`)**: DOS starts Otto with `0` gold and grants `300` gold at London Palace. Remake starts Otto with `startGold: 1000` (`protagonists.ts:62`) for initial fleet provisioning and grants an additional `+300` gold, Letter of Marque (`'33'`), and Knight rank (`promoteRoyalRank: 1`) in `otto.original-core.london-palace-commission`.
   - **Matthew Stowaway Recruitment (`O0`)**: DOS `SNR3` recruits Matthew at London Harbor whether Otto wins or loses the pub duel (`msg#37..65`). In Remake, this is implemented by the single unified event `otto.original-core.london-harbor-matthew-joins` whose trigger accepts `outcomes: ['victory', 'defeat', 'draw']` on `otto.matthew-duel` (no separate `-after-loss` event ID is registered).
   - **Sub-Scene Consolidation (`O1`–`O4`)**: Intermediate rumor/banter beats from DOS `SNR3.MES` (Julio's Lisbon tavern tip, Seville inn search, Ceuta pub banter, royal recall rumor) are consolidated into the dialogue of `seville-pub-intel`, `catalina-pub-alliance`, `london-pub-gold-ship-intel`, `nantes-armada-clash`, and `pub-ezequiel-challenge` so every required naval operation and choice (`london-pub-gold-ship-intel` 3-way choice, `cayenne-amazon-catalina-plea` 2-way choice, `pub-ezequiel-challenge` 2-way choice, `london-palace-triumph` 2-way royal choice) is reachable without dead-end filler stops.
2. **Ernst (`SNR4`)**:
   - **Map Reporting Reward & Fame Scaling (`E0`–`E3`)**: `reportChartedMap()` (`actionsOriginalCore.ts:80-81`) awards **`+80g` and `+35` Adventure Fame per newly reported sector** (`newCells * 80` gold, `newCells * 35` fame). DOS adventure fame thresholds (`1,000` / `5,000` / `20,000` / `40,000`) are scaled to Remake's 10×10 sea-grid scale as `135` (`amsterdam-harbor-laura-joins`), `180` (`hans-zipangu-dream`), `200` (`zeiton-changan-route-unlock`), and `800` (`amsterdam-world-atlas-completion`).
   - **Character & Sub-Scene Consolidation (`E0`–`E2`)**: DOS Paula and the polyglot interpreter are unified in Remake's character catalog as `laura` (`라우라`), whose recruitment (`amsterdam-harbor-laura-joins`), Zipangu discussion (`hans-zipangu-dream`), Zeiton route unlock (`zeiton-changan-route-unlock`), Changan hairpin/residence scenes (`changan-homeland-search`, `changan-home-reunion`), and Nagasaki parents reunion (`nagasaki-parents-reunion`) cover the entire non-combat exploration chain.
3. **Pietro (`SNR5`)**:
   - **Non-Combat Treasure & Discovery Chain (`P0`–`P3`)**: Both treasure maps (`'87'` at Alexandria Pub for `2,000g`/`1,000g`, `'88'` at Venice Pub for `3,000g`/`1,500g`) require actual `'Excavate Treasure'` execution at the destination port Guild (`'49'`,`'7'` Santo Domingo for `'p1-gold-medallion'`; `'77'`,`'7'` Basra for `'p2-poseidons-staff'`) before their story events (`santo-domingo-gold-medallion-excavation`, `basra-staff-excavation`) fire. Ungrounded legacy combats (`pietro.basra-raider`, `pietro.eldorado-fleet`) are excluded from `original-core-v1`.
4. **Ali (`SNR6`)**:
   - **Sultan First Audience & Sasha Reunion Rewards (`A2`–`A3`)**: `istanbul-palace-first-audience` awards **`+3,000g`** (plus Tax Free Permit `'30'`, `promoteRoyalRank: 2`, and `+150` trade fame) instead of DOS raw `50,000g` so that Alexandria's `5,000g` loan to Pietro (`alexandria-pietro-clue`) requires genuine trading profit rather than free palace cash. `basra-sasha-reunion` awards **`+5,000g`**, `+300` trade fame, and `setHowellHousePrice: 50`.
   - **Venice Howell Negotiation Ordering (`A3`)**: `ali.original-core.venice-howell-pietro-debt` requires `completed(ali.original-core.basra-sasha-reunion)` (`events.ts:857`), so in both the source matrix and the user acceptance checklist (`U3`–`U4`) it executes in `A3` after `basra-sasha-reunion`.
